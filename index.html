Support

Spirit is a tea ceremony journal for iPhone: keep your tea collection, run a guided ceremony with infusion timers, and look back on your sessions.

1. Contact us

kurets2001@gmail.com

We answer within 2 business days. Write in English or Russian — both work.

2. Reporting a problem

So we can reproduce the issue, please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the app version (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen);
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.

3. Common questions

How do I delete my account and data? In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete Account. It removes the account and everything stored with it — teas, sessions, and plans. If you cannot reach that screen, email us from the address you signed up with and we will do it for you.

Do I need an account? Yes — your teas and sessions sync to your account so they survive reinstalling the app and follow you to a new device.

Can I suggest a feature? Feature requests are welcome at the same address.
